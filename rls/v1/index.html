The term namespace of the Real Life Stack, the app toolkit: application, data and the connector socket. Permanent identifiers are https://real-life.org/rls/v1#<Fragment>; the fragments resolve to the entries below. Each term is defined by the RLS specification itself; mappings to the other parts come from the shared register, and the dictionary shows everything side by side.

Machine-readable: terms.jsonld (SKOS concept scheme) and index.json.
