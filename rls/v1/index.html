The term namespace of the Real Life Stack, the app toolkit: application, data and the connector socket. Permanent identifiers are https://real-life.org/rls/v1#<Fragment>; the fragments resolve to the entries below.

Each term is defined by the RLS specification itself (repository). Mappings to the other parts of Real Life come from the shared register: a term is same as, close to, related to, broader or narrower than its counterpart, or a deliberate false friend; target: same as marks a convergence the specifications still owe. The dictionary shows all three parts side by side.

Machine-readable: terms.jsonld (SKOS concept scheme, JSON-LD) and index.json (registry with digests).
