The term namespace of the Real Life Network Protocol, the social specification: what a circle, an encounter, a promise, a role mean. Permanent identifiers are https://real-life.org/rlnp/v1#<Fragment>; the fragments resolve to the entries below. Each term is defined by the RLNP specification itself; mappings to the other parts come from the shared register, and the dictionary shows everything side by side.

VisibilitySichtbarkeit#Sichtbarkeit

Not a property of a thing but a consequence of where it lies. Three reaches: within one circle, within several circles, published. Whoever puts a thing into circles decides which ones, and sees beforehand whom it reaches.

EncounterBegegnung#Begegnung

Two people meet, in person or digitally, exchange who they are, and stay connected if it fits. Encounters repeat and are the normal case of the network: a person becomes part of it the moment they enter a relationship.

VerificationVerifikation#Verifikation

The one-time act in which two people confirm that they have met each other as this person, usually at the first encounter; after that, they simply meet. Does not mean general trustworthiness.
