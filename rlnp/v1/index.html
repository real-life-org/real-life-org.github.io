The term namespace of the Real Life Network Protocol, the social specification: what a circle, an encounter, a promise, a role mean. Permanent identifiers are https://real-life.org/rlnp/v1#<Fragment>; the fragments resolve to the entries below.

Each term is defined by the RLNP specification itself (repository). Mappings to the other parts of Real Life come from the shared register: a term is same as, close to, related to, broader or narrower than its counterpart, or a deliberate false friend; target: same as marks a convergence the specifications still owe. The dictionary shows all three parts side by side.

Machine-readable: terms.jsonld (SKOS concept scheme, JSON-LD) and index.json (registry with digests).

#Bezeugen Witnessing · Bezeugen

Definition
Honouring what a person did or contributed. Recognition, not approval. The person witnessed decides about display, not about the statement.
Würdigen, was ein Mensch getan oder eingebracht hat. Anerkennung, keine Abnahme. Die bezeugte Person entscheidet über die Anzeige, nicht über die Aussage.
Also
Confirmation, Attestation

Mappings

  • target: same as Attestation (RLTP)
Target: a general attestation type in RLTP (candidate: DTG Endorsement Credential). Until then only the encounter has an artifact.

#Beziehung Relationship · Beziehung

Definition
A social connection between people, or between people and agents. Arises from encounter, communication, collaboration or shared experience.
Soziale Verbindung zwischen Menschen oder zwischen Menschen und Agenten. Entsteht durch Begegnung, Kommunikation, Zusammenarbeit oder geteilte Erfahrung.
Source
real-life-network-protocol.md#62-beziehung
Mappings
  • broader than Edge (RLTP)
  • target: same as Attestation (RLTP)
  • false friend of Relation (RLS)
Decided 2026-09-18 (for now): an RLNP relationship is broader than an RLTP edge. Two kinds: person–person is an edge from an encounter (RLTP Edge); person–agent is an edge from appointment or vouching (future attestation). Proposal to RLNP 6.2: name both kinds. Relation (Stack) links items, not people: false friend.

#Kreis Circle · Kreis

Definition
A recurring space of encounter. Chooses its own form and access. Circles connect through people, not through parent circles.
Wiederkehrender Begegnungsraum. Wählt Form und Zugang selbst. Kreise verbinden sich durch Menschen, nicht durch übergeordnete Kreise.
Source
real-life-network-protocol.md#64-kreis
Mappings
  • related to Space (RLNP)
A circle is the people, a space is the vessel. A circle chooses its own form and access; a space is meant to carry exactly that: publicly visible, open for anyone to post, or by invitation. Whether the two converge is open (2026-09-18).

#Mensch Person · Mensch

Definition
The smallest unit of the network. Known not by a directory entry but by those who know them. Identity belongs to the person.
Kleinste Einheit des Netzwerks. Kenntlich nicht durch einen Verzeichniseintrag, sondern durch die, die ihn kennen. Die Identität gehört ihm selbst.
Source
real-life-network-protocol.md#61-mensch
Mappings
  • related to Anchor (RLTP)
  • related to Person profile (RLS)
Deliberately no exactMatch: RLTP has no person object, only anchors per context. This is by design (relationships never converge into a person).
The profile is the view of a person, not the person.

#Rolle Role · Rolle

Definition
Responsibility a person takes on toward a circle. A promise that something happens. Voluntary, returnable, not a rank.
Verantwortung, die ein Mensch gegenüber einem Kreis übernimmt. Sagt zu, dass etwas geschieht. Freiwillig, zurückgebbar, kein Rang.
Source
real-life-network-protocol.md#613-rolle
Mappings
  • target: same as Attestation (RLTP)
A role taken on is a witnessed promise (RLNP 6.18, 8.13). Depends on the attestation type.

#Sichtbarkeit Visibility · Sichtbarkeit

Definition
Not a property of a thing but a consequence of where it lies: within a circle, mirrored, or published.
Keine Eigenschaft einer Sache, sondern Folge davon, wo sie liegt: im Kreis, gespiegelt oder veröffentlicht.
Source
real-life-network-protocol.md#616-wie-etwas-sichtbar-wird
Mappings
  • related to Visibility mode (RLTP)
  • related to Mirror (RLS)
  • target: same as Visibility (RLS)
Three ways, three constructions: "within the circle" = RLTP private; "mirrored" = Stack mirror bridge; "published" = RLTP open is only the origin, delivery of open data needs another backend (multi-connector). VisibilityMode covers the first two only.

#Space Space · Space

Definition
A digital or social room for a group, initiative, project or circle. A vessel for shared understanding, not a state of the thing itself.
Digitaler oder sozialer Raum für eine Gruppe, Initiative, ein Projekt oder einen Kreis. Ein Gefäß für Verständigung, kein Zustand der Sache.

#Spiegel Mirror · Spiegel

Definition
A readable copy of a thing in a second place. The thing lives only at its origin; a mirror never writes back. Whoever is to take part needs access to the origin.
Lesbare Kopie einer Sache an einem zweiten Ort. Die Sache lebt nur am Ursprung; ein Spiegel schreibt nie zurück. Wer mitwirken soll, braucht Zugang zum Ursprung.

#Verifikation Verification · Verifikation

Definition
Confirmation of a real encounter or identity relationship. Does not mean general trustworthiness.
Bestätigung einer realen Begegnung oder Identitätsbeziehung. Bedeutet nicht allgemeine Vertrauenswürdigkeit.
