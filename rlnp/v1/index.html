The term namespace of the Real Life Network Protocol, the social specification: what a circle, an encounter, a promise, a role mean. Permanent identifiers are https://real-life.org/rlnp/v1#<Fragment>; the fragments resolve to the entries below.

Each term is defined by the RLNP specification itself (repository). Mappings to the other parts of Real Life come from the shared register: a term is same as, close to, related to, broader or narrower than its counterpart, or a deliberate false friend; target: same as marks a convergence the specifications still owe. The dictionary shows all three parts side by side.

Machine-readable: terms.jsonld (SKOS concept scheme, JSON-LD) and index.json (registry with digests).

EncounterBegegnung

Two people meet, in person or digitally, exchange who they are, and stay connected if it fits. Encounters repeat and are the normal case of the network: a person becomes part of it the moment they enter a relationship.

related to Verification (Network)source real-life-network-protocol.md#81-menschen-kennenlernen

The event itself, repeated and ordinary, deliberately without counterpart in the Stack or the Trust Protocol: the encounter belongs to the people. Verification is the one-time act that records the relationship; later encounters need none.

RelationshipBeziehung

A social connection between people, or between people and agents. Arises from encounter, communication, collaboration or shared experience.

broader than Edge (Trust Protocol)target: same as Attestation (Trust Protocol)false friend of Relation (Stack)source real-life-network-protocol.md#62-beziehung

Decided 2026-09-18 (for now): an RLNP relationship is broader than an RLTP edge. Two kinds: person–person is an edge from an encounter (RLTP Edge); person–agent is an edge from appointment or vouching (future attestation). Proposal to RLNP 6.2: name both kinds. Relation (Stack) links items, not people: false friend.

CircleKreis

A recurring space of encounter. Chooses its own form and access. Circles connect through people, not through parent circles.

also Space, Spacesame as Space (Stack)same as Group (Trust Protocol)source real-life-network-protocol.md#64-kreis

Converged 2026-09-18: a circle is the thing itself; the Stack constructs it as a space, the Trust Protocol as a group. A circle chooses its own form and access, and the space carries exactly that: publicly visible, open for anyone to post, or by invitation. "Space" in the RLNP text is a reference to the Stack space, not a term of its own.

PersonMensch

The smallest unit of the network. Known not by a directory entry but by those who know them. Identity belongs to the person.

RoleRolle

Responsibility a person takes on toward a circle. A promise that something happens. Voluntary, returnable, not a rank.

target: same as Attestation (Trust Protocol)source real-life-network-protocol.md#613-rolle

A role taken on is a witnessed promise (RLNP 6.18, 8.13). Depends on the attestation type.

VisibilitySichtbarkeit

Not a property of a thing but a consequence of where it lies. Three reaches: within one circle, within several circles, published. Whoever puts a thing into circles decides which ones, and sees beforehand whom it reaches.

related to Visibility mode (Trust Protocol)related to Item in several spaces (Stack)target: same as Visibility (Stack)source real-life-network-protocol.md#616-wie-etwas-sichtbar-wird

Three reaches, three constructions: "within one circle" is RLTP private; "within several circles" is the Stack's item in several spaces (the RLTP connector builds it as a mirror); "published" is RLTP open at the origin, delivery of open data needs another backend (multi-connector). VisibilityMode covers the first and third only.

VerificationVerifikation

The one-time act in which two people confirm that they have met each other as this person, usually at the first encounter; after that, they simply meet. Does not mean general trustworthiness.

same as Verification (Stack)same as Enactment (Trust Protocol)related to Encounter credential (Trust Protocol)related to Encounter (Network)source real-life-network-protocol.md#83-menschen-verifizieren

One act in three parts (2026-09-18): the encounter happens in the network, the Stack verifies it in the app, the Trust Protocol constructs that verification as an enactment; the encounter credential is its result. Socially it happens once per relationship; the protocol allows repeated enactments, each with a fresh anchor, all landing on the same edge.
