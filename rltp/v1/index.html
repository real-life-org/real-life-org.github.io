Trust Protocol

https://real-life.org/rltp/v1

The term namespace of the Real Life Trust Protocol, the technical specification: identity, encounter and relationship, access. Permanent identifiers are https://real-life.org/rltp/v1#<Fragment>; the fragments resolve to the entries below. Each term is defined by the RLTP specification itself; mappings to the other parts come from the shared register, and the dictionary shows everything side by side.

The JSON-LD context document is context.jsonld. Per the interim securing profile, credentials pin their @context by value and implementations never process JSON-LD at runtime; this document defines meaning, not machinery.
