The vocabulary namespace of the Real Life Trust Protocol. Permanent term identifiers are https://real-life.org/rltp/v1#<Fragment>; the fragments resolve to the table below.

The JSON-LD context document is context.jsonld. Per the interim securing profile, credentials pin their @context by value and implementations never process JSON-LD at runtime — this document defines meaning, not machinery.

Machine-readable registry: index.json — all terms with IRI and definition, plus context and schema URLs with SHA-256 digests.
