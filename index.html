Real Life consists of three parts. This page shows how they fit together and leads to each.

Real Life Network

A social organism that replicates itself.

Tools for decentralized coordination in real life.

for developers, vibecoders and agents

Real Life Trust Protocol

Decentralized identities and communities grounded in real relationships.

for protocol developers
